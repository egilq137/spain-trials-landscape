Centre duplicates

Every centre key covering more than one row, biggest first. Generated by run_analysis.py. These are candidates: nothing here is merged, and no count in the project depends on this page. Trials are those authorised since 2013.

How to read it. Each bold row is one registry key. Under it, each Site row is a hospital the key resolved into, and the plain rows beneath a site are the spellings that became it. A key with one site was one hospital written several ways. A key with several is either several real sites of one organisation — Institut Català d'Oncologia runs in L'Hospitalet, Badalona and Girona — or unrelated places sharing a name, and merging either would be wrong.

Every total counts each trial once. A key's total can therefore be smaller than its sites added up — a trial running at both Clínica Universidad de Navarra's Pamplona and Madrid sites is one trial for the key and one at each site — and a merged site's total can be smaller than its rows added up, which is the point of merging them: Hospital Clínic's two spellings share two studies, so the site holds 2,925 and not 2,927.

What to look for. A site whose rows are obviously the same place as another site's: that is a merge the rule missed, and the reason is usually in the Town column. Hospital Clínic has a row whose town field holds the hospital's own name, so there was no town to match on. The town is not decisive on its own either — Parc Taulí is filed under Sabadell and under Barcelona, its province.
