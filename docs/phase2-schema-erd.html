Spanish clinical trials registry · Phase 2 schema

Trials Data Model

Twelve tables over 11,847 studies from the AEMPS registry. This revision is written from a field-by-field profile of the whole corpus, replacing a design drawn from a two-year sample. Every count below is measured, not estimated, and the profiles behind them are in docs/profiles/.

What the profile changed

  • Three tables removed. atc_codes and its bridge modelled a field that is empty in all 30,946 intervention records. intervention_administration_routes modelled a many-to-many that never occurs: the route field is pipe-delimited but holds exactly one value every time it is populated.
  • Sponsor and funder identity rebuilt. Deduplicating on the raw name splits AstraZeneca AB from Astrazeneca AB, and Sanofi-Aventis nine ways. Both tables now key on a normalised form and keep the most frequent spelling for display.
  • A centre is a physical site, not a registry reference. One reference covers several campuses, and the reference field itself contains the placeholder NR across 103 different hospitals.
  • No survival columns. The previous design derived censored and a survival window at load. There are three defensible windows and they disagree; the estimand is chosen in analysis, not frozen here.

Entity relationships

studies is the hub. Sponsors own studies one-to-many, because the registry records exactly one sponsor per trial and never a list. Funders, centres and therapeutic areas are each many-to-many and get a bridge. Interventions belong to one study, and only substances repeat within an intervention.

primary key foreign key derived at load, not a raw field undocumented or inferred meaning excluded from the schema

Reading this data without being misled

Four hazards recur across every table. They are properties of the source, not of the schema, and no column can resolve them — but every analysis has to know about them.

absent ≠ zero
A zero usually means "not recorded", not a measured nought. 2,201 studies plan 0 participants; 6,125 (51.7%) set none of the three purpose flags; a study with no funder has no bridge row. Sentinels load as NULL so aggregates skip them rather than averaging them in.
two registries
The identifier format splits the corpus exactly: 6,843 EudraCT and 5,004 CTIS. Funder is recorded for all EudraCT studies and none of the CTIS ones. Substances stop and administration routes start at the same boundary. Several questions can only be asked of one side.
left truncation
Registration begins 2017-11-02, but authorisations run back to 2009 — 3,079 studies (26%) predate the registry. Those appear only if they were still live enough to be entered retrospectively, so early years over-represent long trials. That is selection on the outcome a survival analysis measures.
placeholders
NA is the most frequent acronym (4,744), the most frequent funder name (572), and appears as a centre reference covering 103 different hospitals. Treated as absent everywhere, from an enumerated list rather than a fuzzy match.

Why a centre is a site, not a reference

The registry's referencia looks like a hospital identifier and is not one. Clínica Universidad de Navarra reports Pamplona and Madrid under a single reference; Institut Català d'Oncologia reports Badalona, Hospitalet and Girona under another. Keying on the reference alone left 1,616 study–centre pairs disagreeing about where the trial ran.

Defining the centre as a physical site costs 512 extra rows and removes the problem, rather than pushing it into the bridge key. Geography then lives on centers, where it is stable by construction, and study_centers is a plain two-column bridge. It also keeps 545 Madrid trials in Madrid, which resolving each reference to one region would not have done.

Substances and routes never co-occur. Not one of 30,946 elements carries both: 13,236 have substances only, 17,268 routes only, 442 neither. sustancias runs 97–98% through 2021 and 0% from 2024; viasAdministracion is 0% through 2021 and 96–100% from 2023. The registry swapped one field for the other, and a substance is not a route — so comparability across the transition is genuinely lost, not merely renamed.

Reached by a foreign key on interventions. The source pipe-delimits it, but no populated value ever uses the delimiter for two routes, so the previous design's bridge modelled a relationship the registry does not express. The free text does occasionally name two — oral and iv (43), intravenous (iv) or subcutaneous (sc) (34), 256 rows in all — too few to restore a bridge, but the grouping map has to decide what each becomes.

Excluded on purpose

Everything structured that the endpoint returns is kept, including fields no current analysis question uses, so a later question does not require re-ingesting. Four categories are cut, each for a stated reason.

free text
The 22 informacion fields in two languages — titles, indications, inclusion and exclusion criteria, endpoints. Roughly 80% of the payload's bytes, unstructured, and feeding no question here. Kept in the raw cache: it is what allowed mujer_usa to be interpreted at all.
named individuals
organismo.mail, telefono, personaContacto, and centros[].investigador — confirmed to be real principal-investigator names. Public by legal mandate, so caching them locally is fine, but they never reach the database or the dashboard.
empty or constant
fechaClasificacion and fechaFinPrevista (blank in 11,847/11,847); seven proposito data-source flags (0 in every record); atcs (empty in all 30,946 intervention elements). A column that never varies carries no information.
not returned
intervenciones[].tipo is documented in the AEMPS manual §4.8 and was in the previous revision of this page. The endpoint does not return it. The manual has now proved wrong about a date format, a site-type vocabulary, and a field's existence — live behaviour wins.
